Generated by `scripts/render_tested_models.py` from `research/RESULTS_REGISTER.jsonl`. Nothing on this page is typed by hand except the sentences that say how to read it. If a number here differs from the register, the register is right and this page is out of date.

## What was tested, and what was not

On 2026-11-12 and 2026-11-13 the runtime was run against real hosted models through OpenRouter, with the provider's data-collection setting on `deny` and a spending ceiling per stage. Every stage's rule was written and committed before its run. The runs used one commit (`8772dff4`) and one run per model and prompt; nothing was repeated to improve a result. The screen is short tasks on one date. It does not show that a model keeps working across many runs, it does not compare models with one another, and it says nothing about days of autonomous work, a public host, or users.

Two request settings were used and their results are never combined: `deny + require_parameters` (the provider refuses any endpoint that ignores a parameter we send) and `deny` alone. Several models have no endpoint that supports every parameter, so under `require_parameters` they could not be reached; they are listed in their own table and were screened again under `deny`.

## How to repeat it

The rules, the freeze of the commit and settings, the sealed labels and the stage commands are in `research/PAID_DAY_PLAN_2026-11-12.md` (with its dated amendments), `research/PREPAID_CRITERIA_R103.md` and `research/WORKFLOW_SCREEN_CRITERIA_R113.md`. A run needs your own OpenRouter key set in your own terminal; the tools refuse a key without a credit limit at the provider, and the whole screen of 2026-11-12/13 cost about $5.6.
