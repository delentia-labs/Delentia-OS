# Round two, Amendment 16 (2026-11-18, written before any further call to a second model)

## What the 30-decision probe of nova-pro showed (Amendment 13 note 1, key `delentia-r2m-n2`, Keys page $0.231, provider-reported $0.2308)
Against the rule Q1 (unreadable replies at most 3 of 30, cost per decision at most $0.0030, replies cut at the 2,048-token limit at most 3): **7 of 30 unreadable, $0.0077 per decision, 19 of 42 model calls cut at 2,048 tokens**: Q2 applies, nova-pro is not used for the full R3 tool-choice test.
Read from the raw replies (kept by Amendment 13): every one of the 7 unreadable replies is a goal written in Thai (5 of the 10 Thai goals, 2 of the 10 negatives, which are Thai sentences too); the English set was readable 10 of 10 at $0.0010 per decision. The unreadable replies are runaway text: a field of the reply (the `question` of `delentia_ask_user`, or `reasoning`) turns into Thai characters repeating until the 2,048-token cut, and the loop's retry at 4,096 tokens does the same. Descriptive only, 5 per cell.
So the cause is "nova-pro degenerates when it writes Thai in the loop's JSON reply", not "nova-pro cannot drive the loop". The Thai test needs a model that writes Thai; the English workflows do not.
