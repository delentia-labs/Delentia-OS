# How other agent systems keep memory and context (M1, Round 66)

Written 2026-10-10 from four web searches (standard mode) and the pages they returned. **I read search summaries, not the full pages**; every row says where it comes from and anything the sources disagreed on is marked. Nothing here is a benchmark of those systems: they were not run. Before any of this is used in public copy, open the cited pages and check the version.

## What this says about Delentia's design (my reading, not a measurement)

1. **Nobody in this list wins on byte compression.** Their storage is rows, files, vector stores or snapshots. The comparison that matters is not "who stores fewer bytes" (Round 65 showed a plain zstd stream matches a delta log) but **what history and control the store gives**.
2. **Delentia's combination is different, not necessarily better.** LangGraph keeps every state and lets you fork (history, no revocation, no provenance). Letta lets the agent rewrite its own blocks (control by the agent, not by a person's signature). Mem0 either overwrites or only appends depending on the version. Anthropic's clearing is lossy by design and breaks the cache. A **hash-chained, per-person, revocable event log with provenance** (Round 66, `memory_eventlog.py`) plus **recoverable** tool-output compression (Delta v2 with `delentia_expand_tool_output`) is a different point in that space. Whether it makes agents better at their tasks is exactly what the M-experiments are for.
3. **A cost to remember:** clearing or rewriting what sits at the front of a prompt breaks provider-side caching (Anthropic says so for tool-result clearing). Delentia's read-time policies (`dedupe`, `expire`) change what is offered to recall, which changes the memory block and therefore the prompt; their cost in cache hits has not been measured.
4. **What I could not check from here:** whether LangGraph's checkpointers compress, how Letta audits edits, Mem0's current default, and any independent accuracy comparison. The vendors' benchmark numbers (Mem0 92.5 on LoCoMo, Anthropic +39%) are theirs.
